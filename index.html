FAQ

Common questions

What should I include in my enquiry?

What the cargo is (vehicle make and model, container size or goods), where it is coming from, when it is expected, and where it needs to go in Ghana.

Which documents will I need?

Usually the bill of lading, the commercial invoice or purchase receipt, and your ID or company details. For vehicles we also need the title or export certificate. We confirm the exact list when we quote.

How do I find out the cost?

Each shipment is quoted individually, as clearance, forwarding and haulage depend on the cargo and route. Your quote covers our fees and an estimate of the duty, and the final duty figure is confirmed before release.

How long does clearance take?

It depends on the cargo, the documents and whether customs examines it. Once we have your papers we give you a realistic timeline, and contacting us before the ship arrives is the best way to keep it short.

Can I contact you before my cargo arrives?

Yes, and it is better that way. Talking to us early lets us prepare the documents so clearance starts as soon as the ship docks.

How will I get updates?

On WhatsApp or by phone, at each key stage: arrival, examination, duty payment, release and delivery. You can also ask for an update any time with your shipment reference.
